## Workflow follow-up

- The user reviews and commits; nothing is committed or pushed by the implementation.
- The two-account check against a running server (A blocks B, both reads answer 404, the list, a report, the
  unblock, and the log holding no public id, name, reason or details) is run by hand by the author, as for
  `add-social-profile`; it is not a tracked task.
- Archive the change (`/opsx:archive`) after the user's review.
- The gate of decision 031 is **not** lifted by this change. Before any feature that lists, suggests or searches
  members, and before a public release: the author fills in the reviewer and the review interval in
  `docs/moderation.md`, and the process is in operation.
- The next social feature (Friends, Discover, search or chat) must expose "Block" and "Report" on other
  members' profiles before it is released, verify them on a device, and implement design decision 7 (requests
  and friendships across a block).
